Presenting "Eraser: A Dynamic Data Race Detector for Multithreaded Programs"
Date: 20th Oct 2026

## Details

Contains the details about the presentation, copy pasted from canvas.

Each student will be assigned a presentation group once class enrollment stabilizes. Each group will do one paper presentations at the start of class, after the reading check. These assignments will be shared a few weeks into the semester. Presentations should not summarize the paper - all students have already read the paper. The presentation should be planned for 8-10 minutes and cover these three topics:

- The context of the paper:  reading some related work and understand and present what was going on when the paper was written, and how it advanced over previous ideas. What were the hardware, software or workload changes that lead to this work?
- The impact of the paper: how did the work in the paper influence industry through commercial designs or research, through follow-on projects?
- Questions: what questions does the paper raise and leave unanswered?
